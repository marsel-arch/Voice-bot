Echo

A small voice assistant for commands, weather and stories

Talk to Echo
Press the microphone, or type below.
Try “What time is it?”, “Tell me the weather in Alicante”, or “Tell me a story”.

Weather

Weather appears here. Internet access is needed for a live forecast.

Uses your chosen city and the Open-Meteo forecast service. Forecasts are estimates.

Things you can say

“Hello” / “How are you?”
“What time is it?” / “What’s the date?”
“Tell me the weather in [city]”
“Tell me a story” / “Tell me a funny story”
“Tell me a space story” / “Tell me an animal story”
“Repeat that” / “Stop talking”
“Help” (shows commands)

Voice recognition works in supported browsers and may require internet permission. Allow microphone access when asked.

Echo runs in this page. It does not send your voice to a custom AI service.
